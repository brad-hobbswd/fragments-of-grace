Fragments of Grace

Introduction

“Some wounds leave no visible scars. Some battles happen in silence.”

Some battles are invisible.

Some people carry heaviness they cannot explain. Some live beneath emotional pressure that follows them quietly through ordinary life. Others wrestle with fear, shame, anxiety, exhaustion, addiction, grief, or thoughts that seem to whisper hopelessness into the hidden places of the mind.

Fragments of Grace was written from the understanding that spiritual heaviness is real, but so is healing.

This story follows three siblings navigating emotional wounds, spiritual attacks, painful memories, and years of silent struggle while slowly discovering freedom, restoration, and peace through the name of Jesus.

Their journey is not about perfection.

Their journey is about surrender.

Throughout this story, the image of the alabaster box becomes central. Brokenness is often seen as weakness, but throughout Scripture, brokenness placed before Jesus became something holy. The woman who broke the alabaster box poured out what was precious before the Lord, and Jesus declared her act a memorial that would never be forgotten.

In many ways, this story reflects that same truth.

Every tear, every fragment of pain, every hidden wound, every fearful thought, and every shattered piece of the heart can become something surrendered into the hands of Jesus. Nothing offered to Him is wasted.

This story does not glorify darkness.

This story points toward hope.

Toward healing.

Toward restoration.

Toward the authority and comfort found in the name of Jesus Christ.

For every reader who has ever felt trapped beneath invisible weight, this story was written with you in mind.

Jesus still restores.

Jesus still heals.

Jesus still breaks chains.

And grace still reaches every shattered place.
